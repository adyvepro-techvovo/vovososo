Daynest is an offline daily planner. No account, advertising, analytics, or automatic cloud synchronization. Your tasks and notes stay on your device.

1. About this policy

This policy applies to Daynest for Android, package com.aiavatargenerator.app, version 1.0 (version code 13).

Daynest is provided by YOUR DEVELOPER NAME. For privacy questions, contact adyvepro@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Daynest stores the tasks, dates, priorities, completion status, note titles, and note content that you enter in local app storage. This keeps your planner available between sessions.

We do not receive your planner content, collect it on our servers, sell it, or share it with third parties.

3. Advertising, analytics, and permissions

This version includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not access your advertising identifier or require an account.

Daynest does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone. The Android app does not request internet permission. Its interface is bundled with the app.

4. Exporting and restoring backups

When you export a backup, Daynest creates a JSON file containing your tasks and notes. You choose where to save it using Android’s system document picker.

When you restore a backup, Daynest reads the file you select and replaces your current planner after confirmation. Neither action sends your data to us.

Your chosen document provider may be a cloud storage service. Its handling of your files follows its own privacy policy. Daynest does not encrypt exported backups. Anyone with access to a backup file may be able to read its contents.

5. Retention and deletion

Your planner remains in local app storage until you delete individual entries, choose Settings → Delete all my data, clear app storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Android app backup is disabled for this version. Daynest does not provide automatic cloud synchronization or remote recovery.

Exported backups remain wherever you saved them. Delete those files separately through your file manager or storage provider.

6. Security

Local app storage benefits from Android’s app isolation and your device’s security settings. No storage method is completely secure. Protect your device and keep exported backups safe.

7. Children’s privacy

Daynest is a general-purpose productivity app and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Planner content stays on the user’s device as described in this policy.

8. Platform services

Google Play, Android, and your chosen document providers operate independently of Daynest. They may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

9. This privacy-policy webpage

10. Changes to this policy
